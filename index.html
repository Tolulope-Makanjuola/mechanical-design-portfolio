Incinerator for Medical Waste

A mechanically designed, LPG-assisted medical waste incinerator developed for small- and medium-scale healthcare facilities. The system combines a refractory-lined and ceramic-wool-insulated combustion chamber, controlled air supply, temperature monitoring, ash handling, and a removable particulate filtration unit, with a dedicated exhaust interface for integration with external carbon-capture equipment.

Briquette Press

Designed a hand-operated hydraulic press that compacts biomass waste (sawdust, rice husk, coconut husk) into fuel briquettes, with a load-cell-based system that measures and displays real-time compaction pressure to characterise how different feedstocks respond under load.

03

15 Litres Bio-Ethanol Reactor

Designed a 15-litre stainless steel batch stirred tank bioreactor (STBR) for bio-ethanol production from cassava peel hydrolysate. The project spanned the full mechanical design process — vessel sizing from first principles, pressure wall thickness calculations informed by ASME VIII Div. 1 methodology, dual Rushton turbine impeller and agitation shaft design, instrumentation and nozzle layout, and joining specifications tailored for local Nigerian fabrication.

Automated Cassava Weeder Robot

Designed a 4-wheel autonomous cassava weeding robot, developing the complete mechanical system from concept to fabrication-ready CAD. The project includes a differential drive chassis, rotary blade weeding mechanism, protective shroud, and modular enclosures, supported by detailed 2D technical drawings and a motion animation.

05

Photo-Electrocoagulation Water Treatment Unit

Designed a compact Photo-Electrocoagulation (Photo-EC) system for treating petroleum-contaminated water. The project integrates aluminium electrodes and UV-C treatment in a cost-effective acrylic reactor, with provisions for foam removal, floc filtration, and safe electrical operation. The final design includes a complete CAD assembly, technical drawings, and an operational animation ready for fabrication and testing.

06

Open Channel Flow Teaching Apparatus With Fabricated Supply Tank

This project involved the design of an open channel flow laboratory apparatus through reverse engineering of an existing system. A fabricated supply tank was recreated as a fully parametric SolidWorks model using sheet metal features and assigned AISI 304 stainless steel. The assembly was validated through static simulation under hydrostatic pressure, with detailed 2D drawings, renders, and reports produced for fabrication and documentation.
