01

Low-Cost, AI-Integrated Pick-and-Place Robotic Arm

A desktop five-joint robotic arm that finds objects with a camera and picks them up by name, with no taught positions. An overhead camera and the YOLOE open-vocabulary detector locate each object; a calibrated camera-to-table mapping, inverse kinematics and smooth cubic trajectories then guide the arm. It was designed in SOLIDWORKS, 3D-printed in PLA and built from locally available parts for ₦252,531.

Incinerator for Medical Waste

The system combines a refractory-lined and ceramic-wool-insulated combustion chamber, controlled air supply, temperature monitoring, ash handling, and a removable particulate filtration unit, with a dedicated exhaust interface for integration with external carbon-capture equipment.

04

15 Litres Bio-Ethanol Reactor

Designed a 15-litre stainless steel batch stirred tank bioreactor (STBR) for bio-ethanol production from cassava peel hydrolysate. The project spanned the full mechanical design process — vessel sizing from first principles, pressure wall thickness calculations informed by ASME VIII Div. 1 methodology, dual Rushton turbine impeller and agitation shaft design, instrumentation and nozzle layout, and joining specifications tailored for local Nigerian fabrication.

Photo-Electrocoagulation Water Treatment Unit

Designed a compact Photo-Electrocoagulation (Photo-EC) system for treating petroleum-contaminated water. The project integrates aluminium electrodes and UV-C treatment in a cost-effective acrylic reactor, with provisions for foam removal, floc filtration, and safe electrical operation. 07

Open Channel Flow Teaching Apparatus With Fabricated Supply Tank

This project involved the design of an open channel flow laboratory apparatus through reverse engineering of an existing system. A fabricated supply tank was recreated as a fully parametric SolidWorks model using sheet metal features and assigned AISI 304 stainless steel. The assembly was validated through static simulation under hydrostatic pressure, with detailed 2D drawings, renders, and reports produced for fabrication and documentation.

08

Battery-Powered Grass and Weed Cutter

A cordless grass and weed cutter with a compact wheeled cutting deck, designed in SOLIDWORKS and fabricated in steel. The motor drives the blade directly inside a protective enclosure, an 18 V lithium-ion pack powers it, and the handle angle locks through a spring-loaded Hirth-gear joint that the operator releases with a lever. Designed for a client.
