15 Litres Bio-Ethanol Reactor

Designed a 15-litre stainless steel batch stirred tank bioreactor (STBR) for bio-ethanol production from cassava peel hydrolysate. The project spanned the full mechanical design process — vessel sizing from first principles, pressure wall thickness calculations informed by ASME VIII Div. 1 methodology, dual Rushton turbine impeller and agitation shaft design, instrumentation and nozzle layout, and joining specifications tailored for local Nigerian fabrication. The complete assembly was modelled in SolidWorks, producing detailed part files, fabrication drawings, an exploded view, and a full Bill of Materials for workshop production.
