PROJECT 08

Battery-Powered Grass and Weed Cutter

A cordless grass and weed cutter with a compact wheeled cutting deck, designed in SOLIDWORKS and fabricated in steel. The motor drives the blade directly inside a protective enclosure, an 18 V lithium-ion pack powers it, and the handle angle locks through a spring-loaded Hirth-gear joint that the operator releases with a lever. Designed for a client.

SolidWorks Assembly Design Fabrication Machine Design

Design details

  • Cutting deck: a bolted plate frame on four castor wheels, carrying an enclosure that holds the motor and blade drive. The motor is mounted directly on the cutting head, with an arbor adaptor to the blade.
  • Power and control: a custom 18 V Li-ion pack (5S2P, 21700 cells) and a variable-speed trigger with a safety lock.
  • Handle: a long tubular handle with a middle grip for two-handed control.
  • Angle adjustment: the handle pivots on a shaft with Hirth gears at both ends. A compressed spring keeps the teeth meshed so the handle holds firm, and pulling the side lever releases them to change the angle.
  • Design load: 100 N at the handle grip, 0.45 m from the pivot, gives 45 N·m on the pivot.
